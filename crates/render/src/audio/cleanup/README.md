# Source cleanup

`CleanupChain` runs one item's `project::AudioCleanup` over its source samples inside the mix graph. The chain holds one stage per enabled effect with a nonzero mix. A stage is zero-latency biquad sections (`biquad.rs`, state per channel), spectral noise reduction (`spectral.rs`, with `fft.rs`), de-reverberation (`dereverb.rs`, the same frame layout), the zero-latency de-esser (`deess.rs`) or click removal (`declick.rs`); each keeps its state per channel and continues it across contiguous blocks of the same item. The chain's `latency` is the sum of its stages' delays; the mix graph reads that many samples ahead in the source so the output stays on the requested samples, and `restart`, `push`, `continues` and `fed` are the contract it uses. A chain that does not continue the previous block restarts every stage. Delaying stages lease their state and delay lines from the execution hooks when the chain is built.

The designs, sources and compensation are in [audio](../README.md#source-cleanup). `biquad.rs`, `fft.rs`, `spectral.rs`, `dereverb.rs`, `deess.rs` and `declick.rs` are private to this folder.
