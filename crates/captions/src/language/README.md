# Language profiles

`LanguageRegistry::standard` provides editable profiles for vi, en, bare zh, zh-Hans and zh-Hant. Bare zh uses the Han-character policy. Lookup tries progressively shorter tag prefixes, then the generic profile. The profile's `timing_unit` selects preferred source grouping: words for English and generic, syllables for Vietnamese, characters for Han. UAX #29 keeps mixed Latin and full-width letter runs together while separating Han, and dictionary-protected spans still constrain cue breaks. Profiles select policy without language branches.

Limits are grapheme counts and rates, not bytes. They are initial defaults, not measured reading-speed or font-coverage guarantees, and not a certification of Netflix delivery compliance. Defaults follow Netflix timed-text guides: [Vietnamese](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/220447048-Vietnamese-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) 42 graphemes per line and 17 CPS, [English](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) 42 and 20, [Simplified Chinese](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215986007-Chinese-Simplified-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) and [Traditional Chinese](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215994807-Chinese-Traditional-Timed-Text-Style-Guide) 16 and 9. [General requirements](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements) supply two lines and 5/6..7 seconds, and [timing guidelines](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051554394-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-Subtitle-Timing-Guidelines) the two-frame gap. There is no shot analysis, linguistic reduction or children's/SDH preset.
