# Timeline gestures and zoom

The timeline draws tracks, filmstrip/waveform placeholders, bookmarks and the playhead. An adaptive ruler picks frame/second major and minor ticks from zoom and project fps, labels them with time's shared DF/NDF timecode and never overlaps labels.

Zoom: the exponential slider, ×1.7 buttons and Cmd/Ctrl+wheel share a dynamic fit minimum (content fills 25% of the viewport), a 100× maximum and 75%→15% trailing padding. Zooms above the 15% slider threshold hold the playhead's viewport offset; crossing back below restores the pre-anchor scroll. Wheel scrolls vertically, Shift or horizontal wheel horizontally (following egui content direction); both clamp to content plus padding. Dragging near an edge auto-scrolls in a 100 px zone at up to 15 px per frame, stopping at the content boundary or when the pointer leaves; movement compensates only the clamped shift.

Zoom, scroll, frame-snapped ruler seeking, single and Cmd/Shift multi-selection, move, edge trims, bookmark drag, split, delete and ripple delete dispatch engine commands. Ripple is a persisted toolbar preference (off by default), bindable in Shortcuts, outside app undo history so Undo restores the last project edit. The app passes it into each atomic engine command; gap-closing semantics belong to the [engine ripple policy](../../../../../../crates/engine/src/session/edit/README.md#edit-semantics-and-limits).

Move and edge trim stage the group contract: each pointer update sends absolute `timeline.move_items` or `timeline.trim` params through `timeline.preview`, the panel paints `preview_project()` while the committed project stays byte-identical, and release either publishes one undo step with `timeline.commit_preview` or discards with `timeline.discard_preview` (Escape, gesture reversal, refused candidate). A move drop outlines the landing row or draws an insertion line for a created track; a refusal paints the engine's reason above the scrollbar; a main-track item at the origin is pinned with an accent edge and cannot move left.

Snapping is a persisted preference (on by default) behind the magnet control. It converts 10 logical pixels to ticks at the current zoom and ranks item start/end, playhead, bookmark and keyframe targets by pixel distance, then stable source order. The indicator shows only while a candidate is active. Move, trim and bookmark drag honour the toggle; Shift bypasses it.
